# Why

Delivery riders, salespeople, and field workers collect shop contacts
all day — copying a board's number digit by digit into the phone, often
with dirty hands, often getting one digit wrong.

The board already shows everything: name, numbers, address. The phone's
camera can read it. This tweak closes that gap: one photo, fields
filled, one tap to save. Nothing typed, nothing mis-copied.

Non-goals: card scanners, cloud sync, or storing anything anywhere —
photos stay on the phone and only the final contact is saved, by the
system contacts screen itself.
